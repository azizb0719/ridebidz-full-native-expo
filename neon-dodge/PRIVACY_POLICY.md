# Neon Dodge Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 18, 2026**

Neon Dodge is an Android arcade game published under the package name `com.fourzoya.neondodge`.

## Information Neon Dodge handles

Neon Dodge does not require an account and does not ask users to provide a name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or files.

The game stores gameplay information such as the player's high score locally on the user's device.

## Advertising

Neon Dodge uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertising.

The game may show:

- Optional rewarded ads when a player chooses to watch an ad in exchange for an extra-life continue.
- Interstitial ads at natural breaks between gameplay sessions, such as after a completed run or restart.

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or process information such as:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general or approximate location
- App interactions, including app launches, taps, gameplay-related interactions, and ad or video views
- Diagnostic and performance information
- Device or advertising identifiers where available

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

Data transmitted by Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit.

## Consent and privacy choices

Neon Dodge uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent and provide privacy choices where required by applicable law.

## Children

Neon Dodge is not directed to children under 13. The intended Google Play target audience is age 13 and older.

## Data retention

The player's local high score remains on the device until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Information handled by Google services is subject to Google's own data-retention and privacy practices.

## Third-party services

Neon Dodge uses:

- Google Mobile Ads
- Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

## Data security

Neon Dodge uses commercially reasonable measures provided by the Android platform and its third-party service providers to protect information handled through the app.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Neon Dodge's features, advertising setup, or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on the Neon Dodge Google Play listing.
